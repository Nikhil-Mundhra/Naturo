**Matching Strategies for E-Commerce & Product Search**

* **Attribute Layering:** Combine `[Brand] + [Product Category] + [Variant/Spec]` (e.g., *Nike Air Zoom Pegasus size 10 wide*). These long-tail variations have the highest conversion rates.
* **Match Types to Use (Google Ads / Search Platforms):**
* **Exact Match `[buy running shoes]`:** Use for known, high-converting product SKUs and model numbers.
* **Phrase Match `"best running shoes for men"`:** Captures specific modifier orders while allowing long-tail attributes around the core intent.
* **Negative Keywords:** Proactively exclude service/DIY terms like `repair`, `free`, `jobs`, `how to make`, and `manual`.


To adapt the **`Service × Suburb`** matrix for e-commerce or product-based search, replace geographic modifiers with **product attributes and buyer dimensions**.

### The Product SKAG (Single Keyword Ad Group) Matrix Formula

$$\text{Core Product / Category} \times \text{Primary Modifier} = \text{Product SKAG Universe}$$

Instead of suburbs, your multiplying axes are:

* **Product $\times$ Specific Feature / Spec** (e.g., *Mechanical Keyboard $\times$ Wireless*)
* **Product $\times$ Audience / Use-Case** (e.g., *Running Shoes $\times$ Flat Feet*)
* **Product $\times$ Intent Modifiers** (e.g., *Ergonomic Chair $\times$ Under $200*)

### How to Apply the 3 Tiers to Products

* **Winner SKAGs (High Search Volume & Clear Intent):** Allocate **60–70%** of ad spend. Route users directly to filtered collection pages (e.g., landing page pre-filtered to `/collections/running-shoes?support=flat-feet`).
* **Test SKAGs (Medium / Niche Volume):** Run at low initial budgets using exact match to measure return on ad spend (ROAS).
* **Skip ($< 100$/mo search volume):** Group these into a broader phrase match or standard category shopping campaign rather than building individual dedicated landing pages.
